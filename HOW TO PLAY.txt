ROCKET LEAGUE - FAN REMAKE: HOW TO PLAY
=======================================

Everything runs in the web browser from this folder. No install and no internet needed
(online matches still need internet). Keep the whole folder together: index.html needs
the css, js and assets folders next to it.


CHROMEBOOK
----------
1. Copy RocketLeague-Play.zip to the Chromebook (Google Drive, a USB stick or email).
2. Open the Files app and double-click the zip. It opens like a drive.
3. Copy the RocketLeague-Play folder out of it into "My files" (or Downloads).
   Don't play it from inside the zip or from Google Drive: copy it to My files first.
4. Open the copied folder, right-click index.html > Open with > Chrome.
5. Older or slower Chromebook? Settings > Video > Quality > Very Low.
   (Auto already picks Very Low on most older Chromebooks.)
Tip: bookmark the page in Chrome so next time it's one click.


MAC
---
1. Copy RocketLeague-Play.zip to the Mac and double-click it to unzip.
2. Move the RocketLeague-Play folder wherever you like (for example Documents).
3. Right-click index.html > Open With > Google Chrome (best) or Safari.
4. Safari: if there's no music or sound, click once anywhere on the page.
   Browsers only start sound after a click or key press.
Tip: drag index.html onto the Dock for one-click start.


CONTROLS
--------
W/S drive (and pitch in the air), A/D steer, Space jump (press again with a direction to flip),
Shift or left mouse boost, X or Ctrl powerslide / air roll, Q/E air roll, C ball cam, Esc menu.
Controllers work too (plug in or pair before opening the page). Rebind keys in
Settings > Controls > Configure Controls.


UPDATES
-------
The game never updates by itself: it always plays the version in this folder. To get the newest
version, open Settings > Updates and press UPDATE ZIP FROM GITHUB: it downloads the newest version
and keeps it in the browser. Press DOWNLOAD TO THIS COMPUTER (any time) to save it as a zip in your
Downloads; unzip it and play from that folder instead of this one (your progress stays, it's saved
in the browser). CHECK FOR UPDATES only tells you whether there's a newer version.


SAVES
-----
Progress is saved in the browser on that computer (Chrome and Safari keep separate saves).
Clearing the browser's history and site data clears the save too.
